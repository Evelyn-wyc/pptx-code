# Changelog

## 2026-10-25

Updated the presentation defaults and research workflow from recurring author feedback.

### Slide design

- Specify consistent 30–32 pt bold content titles and normally at least 22 pt bold or semibold body text and key chart labels. Check figure and SVG text at its final slide size.
- Remove the default requirement for a conclusion subtitle. Keep the page-map takeaway as an internal planning statement; add a subtitle only when explicitly requested.
- Prioritize charts, original research figures, and related multi-panel comparisons. Limit visible prose and repeated card layouts; combine sparse pages when they answer the same question.
- Require readable contrast, coordinated colors, comfortable frame padding, and room for line spacing and font differences.
- Use standard paper citations and concise conclusions supported by decisive results.

### Speaker notes

- Keep only natural spoken language, with transitions that follow the argument and the visual reading order.
- Explain every substantive figure, panel, method block, equation, comparison, and conclusion without mechanically reciting all labels or values.
- Explain what each chart shows, why the comparison matters, and what the evidence supports. Keep spoken claims grounded in the current page.
- Move timing estimates, provenance, editing comments, action directions, and duplicate slide-text inventories out of speaker notes.
- Review each slide and its speech together for coverage, consistency, and the available speaking time.

### Revision and validation

- Preserve the latest approved manual edits and synchronize the generator with them.
- Follow the existing project environment and asset structure; generate static slides without animations or transitions by default.
- Strengthen rendered visual review, including cross-platform fonts, embedded figure labels, and the limits of automated geometry checks.
- Synchronize `SKILL.md` and `references/research-ppt-workflow.md`. Keep project-specific palettes, font families, filenames, environments, and page counts configurable.
